How 3D Models Enhance Learning

3D models offer students a physicality to learning concepts not available with traditional methods like photos or technical illustrations. Through in-browser viewers, AR, VR, and 3D printed models, concepts can be replicated exactly as they appear in reality, or abstracted to better illustrate a specific learning objective.

For more information, you can visit the 3D Innovation Hub.

Why You Would Use Them

3D models are an active learning experience. They can be viewed from any angle, and can be pulled apart, animated, and accessed by multiple students concurrently for the same learning experience. Expensive and dangerous pieces of equipment can be shared with no danger of damage or injury. Objects not easily brought into a classroom can be made accessible to students. Narrative experiences can be created to demonstrate a concept or step-by-step process.

How to Use Them in Brightspace or Interactive Panels?

Brightspace and 3D printing are currently the primary methods of interacting with 3D models that we offer. Models can be embedded in Brightspace using Model Viewer running in an iframe to allow students to physically interact with the model. They can also be rendered as static images and added as regular graphics.

We are currently piloting some new AR and VR interactives that can run just from scanning a code, and will hopefully have more information about that soon.
